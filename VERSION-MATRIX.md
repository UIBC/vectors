## How to read this

- **At or above the floor**: the answer must equal the frozen row. A drift here is a defect.
- **Below the floor**: `INCONCLUSIVE` and `FAIL` are both honest — one says "cannot judge",
  the other says "a defect was visible anyway". `PASS` is the only refused answer, because
  a package declared healthy using fields the verifier says it cannot judge is exactly the
  failure the floor exists to prevent.
- `clean` on 0.4.1 is `INCONCLUSIVE`, not `PASS`. That is not a regression; it is the floor
  working, and it is the single most useful cell in this table: an old verifier refuses to
  call the corpus's own positive anchor healthy.
